Common questions

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This removes your account, profile, and any team you captain immediately.

How does matchmaking work?
Create a match request with your team, and FootRank suggests opponents in your city at a similar skill rating and time. Both captains rank their preferred courts, and the app suggests the best match once you accept an opponent.

How is my rating calculated?
Your Pitch Power (PWR) rating updates after each completed, confirmed match based on the result and your opponents' ratings.
