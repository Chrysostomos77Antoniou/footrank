FootRank Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026 · Terms of Service

FootRank ("we", "us", "the app") is a 5-a-side football ranking and matchmaking application. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Chrysostomos Antoniou, trading as FootRank: 5-a-Side Ranking, based in Cyprus. You can contact us about anything in this policy at tomisapoelcity@gmail.com. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as we are not required to.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, username, email address, and (if you choose) profile photo. When you sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from that provider to create your account.
  • Contact information: your mobile number, which is required to create an account. It's used to keep one account per person, and it's shared only with the captain of a team you have a confirmed match against, so the two of you can coordinate. Each mobile number can only be linked to one FootRank account.
  • Profile & gameplay data: preferred position, city, team membership, match results, ratings (Pitch Power), reliability and behavior scores.
  • Content you create: teams, match requests, court preferences, scores, and behavior reports.
  • Payment information: when a match fee is paid, we store the amount, currency, payment status, and Stripe's payment reference. Your card details are entered into Stripe's secure payment form and handled by Stripe; we do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Device and diagnostic data: a push-notification token for your device, and crash reports (for example device model, operating system version, app version, and technical details of the error) that help us find and fix bugs.
  • Record of your agreement: the date and version of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy you accepted.

How we use your information, and our legal basis

  • Create and manage your account and profile; match teams, schedule matches, suggest a court, calculate rankings, and share your mobile number with the opposing captain. Legal basis: performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Take payment of match fees. Legal basis: performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Send match-related notifications (reminders, invitations, results). Legal basis: performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Keep the service fair and secure (dispute handling, preventing fraud and rating manipulation). Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Find and fix crashes and operate and improve the app, including automated analysis of aggregate app data. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Keep proof that you accepted our Terms and Privacy Policy. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in being able to show what was agreed (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Providing the information marked as required (name, email, mobile number) is necessary to create an account. Without it we cannot provide the service.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your name, photo, position, city, team, and ratings are visible to other FootRank users as part of the app's social and ranking features. We use the following service providers (processors) to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage. Our database is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications and crash reporting.
  • Stripe — payment processing for match fees.
  • Google, Apple, and Facebook — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Anthropic — our internal operations dashboard uses Anthropic's Claude models to help us analyze app data (e.g. usage trends, data quality, dispute patterns) so we can run and improve the service. This is an internal tool operated by us, not a public-facing feature, and is not used to make automated decisions about individual users.

Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some of these providers (for example Google, Stripe, and Anthropic) may process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer relies on a safeguard required by Chapter V of the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for details of the safeguards that apply by contacting us.

Automated decisions

Your Pitch Power rating and reliability and behavior scores are calculated automatically from match results and reports. When two captains submit conflicting scores, the app applies an automated resolution process that can result in a rating penalty for the captain found responsible for a false report (see section 7 of the Terms of Service). If you think an automated outcome is wrong, you can ask for a person to review it, tell us your side, and contest the decision by emailing us. We will respond within one month.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to receive your data in a commonly used format. You can correct your profile in the app, and delete your account and data yourself at any time from Profile → Delete Account. For a copy of your data or any other request, email us at the address below; we will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Cyprus this is the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (dataprotection.gov.cy). We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first, but you are not required to contact us before complaining.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account yourself at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account), which removes your account, profile, and any team you captain immediately. You can also request deletion by contacting us at the email below; we will remove your personal data within 30 days.

Your record of accepting the Terms and Privacy Policy is deleted together with your account. Stripe keeps its own records of payments as required by financial regulations.

What we keep after you delete your account

When an account is deleted we retain one limited record, so that penalties and sanctions can't be avoided simply by deleting an account and signing up again. That record contains:

  • A one-way, salted cryptographic hash of your email address and mobile number. We do not keep the email address or the number itself. A hash cannot be read, dialled, emailed, or used to contact you — it can only be compared against a value someone enters later to tell whether that identity has been used on FootRank before.
  • Your final gameplay standing: Pitch Power, reliability and behavior scores, matches played, dispute count, and whether the account was flagged.

If you later create a new account with the same email address or mobile number, that standing is restored to the new account. It is only ever applied in a way that can't improve the new account's position — an account can never gain an advantage from being deleted and recreated.

Legal basis. This is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in preventing fraud and abuse and in keeping competition fair, as recognised in Recital 47 GDPR. We keep only a hash rather than the underlying details so that the data is the minimum needed for this purpose.

Retention period. This record is automatically and permanently deleted 24 months after the account is deleted.

Your rights. You may object to this processing under Article 21 GDPR by contacting us at the email below. Note that because this record exists specifically to prevent evasion of penalties, we may decline an objection where there are compelling legitimate grounds to keep it — for example, where the account was flagged or sanctioned. If your account was in good standing, we will remove the record on request.

Security

Access to your data is protected with industry-standard authentication and row-level security. Sign-in is handled by trusted providers (Supabase Auth, Google, Apple, Facebook). If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify the Cyprus Commissioner and, where the law requires it, you.

Age requirement

FootRank is intended for users aged 16 and over (see our Terms of Service for the full eligibility rule). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. You confirm you are 16 or over when you accept these documents in the app.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly. If we make a material change, we will ask you to review and accept it again in the app.
