# FootRank Social Promo Design

## Goal

Create a short, vertical promotion for Instagram Reels and TikTok that makes
FootRank feel competitive, cinematic, and energetic.

## Deliverable

- One 12-second, 9:16 MP4 video suitable for social posting.
- No dialogue or baked-in music, so it can be paired with platform-native
  trending audio.

## Creative sequence

1. **0–3 seconds:** An evening five-a-side pitch under floodlights. A football
   drops into frame and lands with impact. On-screen text: “Your next match is
   out there.”
2. **3–7 seconds:** Rapid, cinematic action cuts: sprinting players, a tackle,
   a shot, a goal, and celebration. On-screen text: “Find opponents. Play for
   real.”
3. **7–10 seconds:** A luminous ranking/leaderboard visual surges upward over
   the action. On-screen text: “Win. Climb your Pitch Power.”
4. **10–12 seconds:** A dark-green FootRank brand end card. On-screen text:
   “FOOTRANK” and “Own the pitch.”

## Visual direction

- Vertical 9:16 framing, fast cuts, high contrast, deep green accents, and
  authentic five-a-side football energy.
- Players are generic adult athletes; no real clubs, players, or trademarks.
- The generated video is brand-led rather than a literal recording of the app.

## Acceptance criteria

- The FootRank value proposition is comprehensible without sound.
- Every message remains readable on a phone.
- The final frame provides a clean, recognisable FootRank call to action.
